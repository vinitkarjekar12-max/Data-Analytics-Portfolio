# Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes sales, cost, and operational data for Nassau Candy Distributor to identify the products, divisions, and suppliers driving profitability. The objective was to uncover margin risks, profit concentration, and opportunities for business growth through data-driven decision making.

## Business Problem

The company lacked visibility into:

* Which products generate the highest profit
* Whether high-selling products are truly profitable
* Division-wise profit contribution
* Margin-risk products affecting profitability
* Supplier and regional dependency risks

## Tools & Technologies

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Streamlit

## Analysis Performed

* Data Cleaning & Validation
* Product Profitability Analysis
* Gross Margin Analysis
* Division Performance Evaluation
* Pareto (80/20) Analysis
* Cost vs Sales Diagnostics
* Supplier & Regional Risk Assessment
* Interactive Dashboard Development

## Key Insights

* Top 5 products contributed approximately 92% of total sales and profit, indicating high business concentration risk.
* Wonka Bar – Scrumdiddlyumptious emerged as the highest profit-contributing product.
* The Chocolate Division generated over 95% of total company profit.
* Everlasting Gobstopper achieved the highest gross margin (80%) but showed low sales volume.
* Kazookles had the lowest profitability with only 7.69% gross margin, making it a strong candidate for pricing review or discontinuation.
* The business showed heavy dependence on two suppliers and two major regions, creating operational and supply-chain risks.

## Recommendations

* Increase investment in high-margin products.
* Optimize pricing and procurement costs for low-margin products.
* Diversify product portfolio to reduce revenue concentration.
* Expand supplier network to lower dependency risk.
* Leverage customer analytics for targeted marketing and demand forecasting.

## Business Impact

The analysis identified profitability drivers, margin improvement opportunities, supplier risks, and portfolio diversification strategies, enabling management to make more informed and data-driven business decisions.
