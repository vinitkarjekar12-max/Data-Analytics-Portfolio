# Cost Structure & Channel-Wise Profitability Analysis for Multi-Channel Restaurants

## Overview

This project analyzes the profitability of restaurant sales channels including In-Store, Self-Delivery, Uber Eats, and DoorDash. The goal was to identify which channels truly drive profit, quantify margin erosion caused by commissions and delivery costs, and recommend strategies for sustainable growth.

## Business Problem

Many restaurant businesses focus on revenue growth without understanding whether individual sales channels are actually profitable. This analysis investigates the impact of platform commissions, delivery costs, and operating expenses on overall profitability.

## Tools & Technologies

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Tableau, Streamlit

## Analysis Performed

* Channel-wise Profitability Analysis
* Margin & Cost Structure Analysis
* Commission Impact Assessment
* Delivery Cost Optimization
* Segment-wise Performance Evaluation
* ROI & Risk Analysis
* Scenario Simulation using Streamlit

## Key Insights

* Uber Eats and DoorDash generated 61% of total revenue but contributed only 5% of total profit.
* In-Store and Self-Delivery channels generated nearly 95% of company profits, making them the true profit drivers.
* High aggregator commissions (30%+) were the primary cause of margin erosion.
* A 5% reduction in commission rates could increase aggregator profit contribution from 6% to 27%.
* Full-Service Restaurants showed negative margins on aggregator platforms, while Ghost Kitchens remained profitable across all channels.
* A negative correlation between aggregator order volume and profitability indicated that increasing orders was actually increasing losses.

## Recommendations

* Prioritize In-Store and Self-Delivery channels for long-term growth.
* Renegotiate commission structures with delivery aggregators.
* Reprice menu items on aggregator platforms to protect margins.
* Shift customers from aggregators to owned channels through loyalty programs and incentives.
* Expand high-performing Ghost Kitchen operations and optimize underperforming restaurant segments.

## Business Impact

The analysis helped uncover hidden profitability issues, quantify the financial impact of aggregator commissions, and provide a data-driven strategy for improving margins. The project was further enhanced through Tableau dashboards and a Streamlit application that enables real-time profitability scenario analysis.
